Handmade leather wallets

Every wallet we make starts as a single hide of full-grain vegetable-tanned leather sourced from a small tannery that has worked the same way for over a century. We cut each piece by hand, burnish the edges with beeswax, and saddle-stitch every seam with waxed linen thread so that it will not unravel even if a single stitch is cut. The result is a wallet that softens and darkens with use, developing a patina that is unique to the person who carries it. Our bifold holds up to eight cards and a fold of notes without bulging, while the slim card holder is designed for people who carry only the essentials. Both are available in natural, brown and black, and both come with a lifetime repair guarantee: if a seam ever fails we will restitch it for free. We believe that good things should last, and that repairing something is almost always better than replacing it. That is why we publish our care guide openly, sell replacement thread at cost, and run repair workshops twice a year in our studio. Leather is a natural material, so small marks and variations in grain are part of its character rather than defects. Keep your wallet away from prolonged direct heat, condition it once or twice a year with a neutral balm, and it will reward you with decades of service. Shipping is free on orders over fifty dollars, and every order is packed in recycled cardboard with no plastic. If you are not completely happy with your purchase, you can return it within sixty days for a full refund, no questions asked. We also offer free monogramming on every wallet, with up to three letters stamped by hand using traditional brass letter punches heated on a small gas flame in the workshop. Many customers choose initials for themselves, but monograms are also a popular way to turn a wallet into a thoughtful personal gift for a birthday, graduation, anniversary or retirement celebration later this year. Our team answers every email personally, usually within one working day, and we are always happy to help you choose the right size, colour or style for the way you actually carry your cards and cash day to day.
